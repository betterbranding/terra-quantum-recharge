Overnight Sessions

Planning your stay

How long are the overnights, what should I bring, and when do they start?

Check in between 8:45 and 9:00 PM. If you're arriving after 9:00 PM or running late, let us know in advance so we can let you in; we lock the door at 9:00 PM. Our on-site attendant will be there to monitor. If you're flying in and your flight is delayed, please text us at 512-444-2007.

Overnight attendees must bring a blanket and pillow. If you're paying for a bed, you must bring a fitted sheet sized for your bed of choice; there's a $15 fee for those who don't bring sheets.

Some clients like to bring noise-canceling headphones, as we play 432 and 528 Hz frequency music throughout the night. Wake-up is by 7:00 AM at the latest (earliest wake time is 6:30 AM so as not to disrupt others), and leave time is 8:00 AM at the latest.

How should I prepare for my session?

Avoid stimulants like caffeine or ginseng before your session. Stay hydrated and maintain your electrolyte balance for the best experience. Arrive with an open heart and mind, ready to relax and support your body's natural healing. Remember to bring a blanket, water, and noise-canceling headphones if you'd like them.

How often should I have a session?

If you're new to the Energy Enhancement System, aim to build up to 20 hours minimum, even if that takes several weeks or months. You don't want too much time to pass between sessions if you have considerable wellness goals; 2+ hour sessions are recommended for the most benefit.

As Dr. Sandra Rose Michael says, "More is better." You cannot spend too much time in an EMF-free zone, and the benefits are cumulative, so the more you come, the better you may feel.
